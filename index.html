About

Jack McElwee is a news reporter for The Arbiter, Boise State University's independent student newspaper.

Jack's reporting follows how federal programs, university decisions and the weather play out on campus and across Boise: an $8 million federal grant to keep new teachers in Idaho classrooms, opportunity zones that could open campus to new development, a Labor Day flood that pushed students and classes out of the engineering building, and a record-low snow year at Idaho's ski resorts.

Alongside breaking news, Jack writes about the people and places behind campus life, from the student-run hall councils in the residence halls to the history inside the Communication Building, built in 1942.
