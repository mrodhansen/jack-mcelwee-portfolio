Communication Building leans into Idaho History for 2026 Haunted House

The haunted house is back at Boise State’s Communication Building, put on by students from the Communications and Media Departments.

By Jack McElwee · The Arbiter

The haunted house is back at Boise State’s Communication Building.

The event is a collaborative effort between students from the Communications and Media Departments and is led by Dr. Sarah Silverman and Nathan Snyder.

This year’s haunted house will be themed around a historic – and haunted – Idaho mining town.

“We’re focusing on Silver City, which is a haunted ghost town in Idaho,” Silverman said. “So [the communications students] have gone out and gathered stories, and they’re going to take those stories and recreate them in the building. And then [media students are] going to help us bring it all to life with sound and lights and all the technology side of things.”

This is the second year the Communication Building has hosted a haunted house. Silverman described last year’s rendition as an “amalgamation of different haunted places around Boise.”

Dinah, the ghost that lives in the building according to campus urban legend, served as the tour guide for last year’s haunted house.

When asked if Dinah would be incorporated into the tour this year, Silverman was noncommittal, but did say she thinks Dinah “will make an appearance.”

Although it is a university production, the haunted house will feature elements similar to traditional haunted houses. There will be jump scares, actors, light and sound and fog machines, according to Silverman.

“I would say at least PG-13,” Silverman said when asked how “scary” the haunted house would be.

Admission is completely free, however, Silverman said the departments are accepting donations. Last year’s event drew around 300 people, and she anticipates a similar number this year.

“I would encourage people to come,” Silverman said. “It’s a novelty and such a cool thing that we get to do this on our campus. And in order for things like this to continue happening on our campus, we have to show support.”

The Haunted House will take place from 7-10 p.m. on Friday, Oct. 30.
